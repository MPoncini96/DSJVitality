# Data sources

Every metric in this prototype carries a provenance tag:

- **REAL**: taken from a public source listed below. Belongs to a real school.
- **SAMPLE** (shown as "Simulated"): generated by `scripts/generate_sample.ts` (fixed seed `20261016`). Belongs only to the 25 fictional demo schools (School A–Y, `sample/demo_schools.csv`). Not real figures for any school, and not modeled on any school.

REAL files live in `data/`. SAMPLE files live in `data/sample/`. `lib/data.ts` refuses a SAMPLE row for a real school and a REAL row for a demo school.

## SAMPLE: the demo system

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `sample/demo_schools.csv` | 25 fictional schools, `demo-a`..`demo-y`, with a random size band. |
| `sample/enrollment.csv`, `sample/births.csv` | Ten years of K–8 enrollment and 20 years of births near each demo school. Kindergarten is a drifting share of births five years earlier. |
| `sample/tuition.csv`, `sample/budget_actual.csv`, `sample/cash_monthly.csv`, `sample/receivables.csv`, `sample/tuition_assistance.csv` | Finance. Shrinking demo schools tend to run tighter budgets. No public source exists for any of this. |
| `sample/facilities.csv` | Assets per school. Each school's facility condition index is drawn from 8–45%, so a Facilities score of 100 can't occur. |
| `sample/pipeline.csv`, `sample/reenrollment.csv`, `sample/data_audit.csv` | Admissions, re-enrollment and data feeds. |
| `sample/turnaround.csv`, `sample/turnaround_actions.csv` | Program roster (the five weakest demo schools on a simple screen) and action plans. |

The real schools get no simulated data. Where a real school has no public figure (budgets, re-enrollment, facilities), nothing is shown. The 8 high schools and order-run/independent schools in the directory are in `schools.csv` but carry no data yet.

## Reference dates

The latest school year is read from `enrollment.csv` (2025-26, published by CDE in Sept 2026; the publication month is `CDE_PUBLISHED` in `lib/config.ts`). The demo system uses the same school year; its internal data is a snapshot as of **October 1, 2026** (`DEMO_AS_OF`): FY2026 (July 2025–June 2026) is closed and FY2027 has three months of actuals.

## Known issues in the REAL enrollment (also in `match_review.csv`)

- **St. Joseph of Cupertino** has no record in the 2022-23 file. System totals carry its 2021-22 value forward for that year, and the chart says so.
- **St. Martin of Tours and St. Nicholas, 2019-20:** the reported total is higher than the sum of grades K–8 (by 31 and 19), most likely TK or ungraded students. The dashboard uses the K–8 grade sum everywhere.
- **Large one-year swings** (St. Joseph of Cupertino 2021-22→2023-24, St. Justin 2023-24→2024-25, St. Lawrence 2021-22→2022-23) are reported as filed; worth confirming with the schools.
- Names differ between the directory and CDE for several schools (e.g. "St. Lawrence Elementary and Middle School" vs "St. Lawrence School"). Matching is by CDS code, not name.

## Scenarios (labeled "Scenario, not a forecast")

Computed in `lib/metrics.ts` from REAL inputs for each school through the last year births allow (births 2024 → kindergarten 2029-30). Two scenarios bracket a range:

- **Trend:** latest enrollment × (1 + annual change since 2019-20, capped at −6% to +4%) per year.
- **Births:** latest enrollment × the average, over grades K–8, of births for the cohort in that grade that year ÷ births for today's cohort in that grade.

Shown as low–high only, never as one number, and never in alerts. The system range on the Radar is the sum of the schools' lows and highs.
